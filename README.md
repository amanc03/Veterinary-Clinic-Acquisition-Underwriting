# Veterinary-Clinic-Acquisition-Underwriting
# Veterinary Clinic Acquisition Underwriting

*Private Markets | Acquisition Underwriting | Real Assets | Investment Analysis*

> Project completed at **Lionchase North America** as a **Fund Advisory Intern**, supporting underwriting and due diligence for a **live ~$20M veterinary hospital acquisition** within the **Acxtus Fund**.

---

## Overview

The objective was to support underwriting and due diligence on a live veterinary hospital acquisition by evaluating the property's economics, operating performance, expansion potential, and redevelopment alternatives.

The analysis combined the characteristics of the underlying real estate with the economics of the operating business to assess the transaction from both an **asset and operating-company perspective**.

The broader objective was to determine whether operational improvements, capacity expansion, and asset repositioning could strengthen the investment case and create additional value.

---

## Business Problem

The transaction involved more than acquiring a physical property. Investment viability depended on understanding how the veterinary operation, facility capacity, redevelopment requirements, and future operating economics interacted.

Key questions included:

* What were the economics of the existing property and operating business?
* Where were the largest operating-cost pressures?
* Could the facility support additional capacity and service-line growth?
* What redevelopment alternatives could improve asset utilization?
* How would operating and property improvements affect future cash flow?

The underwriting process therefore required evaluating the asset as an **integrated operating and real-estate investment** rather than viewing the property independently from the business occupying it.

---

## What I Built

I supported the underwriting process by:

* **Analyzing property economics and operating costs** to evaluate the existing asset and business structure
* **Assessing capacity expansion and service-line opportunities** to identify potential operating upside
* **Evaluating redevelopment alternatives and asset-repositioning strategies** alongside architecture and management teams
* **Developing a five-year financial forecast** connecting operating assumptions with future financial performance
* **Supporting investor and fund-management discussions** around capital requirements, portfolio considerations, and value-creation opportunities

The work connected physical asset decisions with the future economics of the underlying veterinary operation.

---

## Business Logic

The investment thesis depended on whether improvements to the physical asset and operating model could produce stronger future economics.

The analytical relationship was:

**Existing Asset → Operating Performance → Capacity / Redevelopment → Cost Structure → Cash Flow → Investment Value**

Capacity expansion could create additional revenue opportunities, while redevelopment and operating improvements could reduce structural cost pressures.

The underwriting therefore focused on the relationship between:

* **Property economics**
* **Operating expenses**
* **Facility capacity**
* **Redevelopment requirements**
* **Future cash-flow potential**

This allowed the transaction to be evaluated through both its current economics and its potential for **operational improvement and asset repositioning**.

---

## Analytical Implementation

### 1. Asset & Operating Review

Property characteristics, operating costs, capacity requirements, and existing business conditions were reviewed to establish the current economics of the investment.

The analysis focused on identifying the key variables affecting both property utilization and operating performance.

### 2. Underwriting & Scenario Analysis

Operating assumptions and redevelopment alternatives were evaluated to determine how potential changes could affect future economics.

The analysis incorporated:

* Property economics
* Operating-cost structure
* Capacity expansion
* Service-line growth
* Redevelopment alternatives
* Asset repositioning

These assumptions formed the basis of the five-year financial outlook.

### 3. Investment Review

The resulting analysis supported discussions with **Acxtus management, investors, and architecture teams** regarding capital requirements, redevelopment strategy, operating improvements, and transaction viability.

The work provided a structured view of how operational and physical-asset decisions could affect the investment thesis.

---

## Business Impact

The underwriting connected **property economics, operating performance, and redevelopment strategy** within a single investment framework.

The analysis supported an asset-repositioning and operating-improvement plan that contributed to a **10% reduction in OPEX**.

**Property + Operations → Value Creation → Cash Flow → Investment Decision**

The broader value of the project was its ability to evaluate the veterinary hospital as both a real asset and an operating business, providing investors with a clearer view of the transaction's risks, operating upside, and value-creation potential.
